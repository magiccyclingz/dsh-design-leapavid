# Linear · Linear

Linear's marketing canvas is the deepest dark surface in this collection — `#010102` is #010102, essentially pure black with a faint blue tint. On top sits a four-step surface ladder (`#0f1011` through `#191a1b`) for cards, panels, and lifted tiles, with hairline borders running from `#23252a` (#23252a) up through `#34343a` and `#3e3e44`.…

- 画布 canvas `#010102` · 表面 surface `#141516` · 描边 `#232424`
- 文字 ink `#f7f8f8` · 次要 `#d0d6e0` · 强调 accent `#5e6ad2`（其上文字 `#ffffff`）
- 字体 display `Linear Display` 600/-3px · body `Linear Text`
- 圆角 card 8px / button 9999px / pill 9999px · 间距基准 8px

## 版式与间距
- Base unit: 4px.
- Tokens (front matter): spacing.xxs 4px · spacing.xs 8px · spacing.sm 12px · spacing.md 16px · spacing.lg 24px ·…
- Card interior padding: spacing.lg 24px on feature/pricing cards; spacing.xl 32px on testimonial cards; spacing.xxl 48px…
- Pill button padding: 8px vertical · 14px horizontal — Linear's compact button spec.
- Form input padding: 8px vertical · 12px horizontal.

## 形状
- Product UI screenshots dominate; they sit in rounded.xl 16px tiles with spacing.lg 24px outer padding.
- Customer logo tiles render at small sizes (~24px logo height) on colors.canvas with no border.
- Avatar circles in testimonial cards use rounded.full at 32–40px sizes.

## 该做
- Reserve colors.canvas (#010102) as the system's anchor surface — the faint blue tint is intentional.
- Use colors.primary lavender ONLY for: brand mark, primary CTA, focus ring, link emphasis.
- Use the four-step surface ladder for hierarchy. Avoid skipping levels.
- Pair display weight 600 with body weight 400 — Linear resists 700+ display weights.

## 避免
- Apply negative letter-spacing aggressively on display.
- Use product UI screenshots as the protagonist of every section.
- Compose CTAs as rounded.md 8px corners.
- Don't ship a light-mode marketing page.
