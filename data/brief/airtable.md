# Airtable · Airtable

Airtable's marketing surfaces are quietly editorial. The base atmosphere is white canvas, dark ink type, generous whitespace, and a near-black pill CTA — nothing is fighting for attention until a section needs to. The brand voltage doesn't come from gradient washes or accent walls; it comes from full-bleed signature cards in `#aa2d00`, `#0a2e0e`, and …

- 画布 canvas `#ffffff` · 表面 surface `#f6f6f6` · 描边 `#dfdfe1`
- 文字 ink `#181d26` · 次要 `#898c90` · 强调 accent `#2d7ff9`（其上文字 `#ffffff`）
- 字体 display `Haas Groot Disp` 500/0px · body `Haas`
- 圆角 card 10px / button 9999px / pill 9999px · 间距基准 8px
