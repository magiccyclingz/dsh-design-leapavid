# ElevenLabs · ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs reads like a quietly editorial print magazine that happens to be a voice-AI product. The base canvas is off-white `#f5f5f5` (#f5f5f5) holding warm near-black ink (#0c0a09). The brand voltage is photographic, not chromatic: soft pastel atmospheric gradient orbs (mint, peach, lavender, sky, rose) drift through the page as the only "color" moments. There is no neon accent, no…

- 画布 canvas `#f5f5f5` · 表面 surface `#ededed` · 描边 `#d4d4d4`
- 文字 ink `#0a0a0a` · 次要 `#7b7b7b` · 强调 accent `#292524`（其上文字 `#ffffff`）
- 字体 display `Waldenburg` 300/-1.92px · body `Inter`
- 圆角 card 8px / button 9999px / pill 9999px · 间距基准 16px

## 版式与间距
- Base unit: 4px.
- Tokens: spacing.xxs 4px · spacing.xs 8px · spacing.sm 12px · spacing.base 16px · spacing.md 20px · spacing.lg 24px…
- Section padding: 96px.
- Max content width: ~1200px.
- Editorial body: 12-column grid.

## 该做
- Reserve colors.primary (ink pill) for primary CTAs.
- Use Waldenburg Light at weight 300 for every display headline. Never bold.
- Use Inter at +0.15-0.18px tracking for body — the editorial dialect.
- Use atmospheric gradient orbs (mint/peach/lavender/sky/rose) as decoration only.

## 避免
- Use the pill shape for every CTA and badge.
- Don't introduce a saturated brand action color. Ink pill is the only CTA color.
- Don't bold display copy. Display sits at weight 300 — bolding shifts the brand voice from editorial to consumer-marketing.
- Don't use gradient orbs as button fills, text colors, or component backgrounds. They are pure atmosphere.
